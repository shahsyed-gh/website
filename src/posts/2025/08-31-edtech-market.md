# Product Strategy in the Generative EdTech Gold Rush

Ithaka S+R's extensive tracking of over 100 generative AI tools in higher education presents a sobering reality for EdTech product managers: market saturation is driving toward inevitable consolidation. The research reveals three distinct product categories dominating the landscape: discovery tools that help users find relevant academic content, understanding tools that summarize and synthesize materials, and creation tools that generate text, images, or code. For product managers, the critical insight is that most offerings are becoming commoditized apps running on OpenAI's GPT infrastructure, making differentiation increasingly challenging. The study found that 50-65% of students and faculty already use ChatGPT or competitors, suggesting the market has reached early majority adoption faster than traditional EdTech products.

The path forward for product managers lies in strategic positioning and platform integration rather than feature differentiation. Major scholarly publishers like Elsevier and JSTOR are leveraging their content moats to build comprehensive workflows spanning discovery through creation, while learning management systems like Blackboard and D2L are embedding AI features directly into existing user workflows. Product managers should focus on either securing unique data advantages or integrating into established platforms where users already spend their time. The research warns that standalone tools without clear differentiation face acquisition, failure, or marginalization as OpenAI's dominance continues to reshape the competitive landscape, making strategic partnerships and platform plays essential for survival.

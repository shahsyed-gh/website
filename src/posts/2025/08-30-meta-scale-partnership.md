# When Billion-Dollar Partnerships Hit Reality

Meta's $14.3 billion investment in Scale AI is showing cracks just two months in, with key executives departing and internal teams questioning data quality. For product managers building AI-powered products, this situation illuminates the critical balance between strategic vendor partnerships and maintaining operational flexibility. The fact that Meta's TBD Labs researchers prefer competitors Surge and Mercor over their own billion-dollar investment partner reveals how product quality trumps financial relationships when building cutting-edge AI systems. This disconnect between investment strategy and operational reality should serve as a warning for PMs evaluating AI vendor partnerships.

The Scale AI situation highlights a fundamental product management principle: vendor consolidation carries hidden risks that can derail product roadmaps. Scale AI's traditional crowdsourcing model worked for basic data labeling, but modern AI products require domain experts like doctors and scientists to generate high-quality training data. Product managers should evaluate whether their AI vendors can scale expertise alongside their technology, not just processing volume. Meta's diversification strategy with multiple data providers, despite their massive Scale AI investment, demonstrates smart risk management that product teams should emulate when building mission-critical AI features.

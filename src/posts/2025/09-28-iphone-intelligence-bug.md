# Launch Day Reality Check: When AI Features Fail

Apple is addressing a bug preventing some iPhone 17 users from downloading Apple Intelligence, leaving core AI features like Genmoji, Image Playground, and Writing Tools inaccessible on new devices. The issue affects a small number of users who report that Apple Intelligence either fails to download completely or stops working after initially functioning properly. Apple support has acknowledged the problem and is working on a fix, though it remains unclear whether the solution requires a server-side adjustment or a full iOS update.

For product managers, this situation illustrates the unique challenges of launching AI-powered products where features depend on large model downloads and continuous server connectivity. The intermittent nature of the bug, where some users report initial functionality followed by sudden failure, highlights how AI product reliability differs from traditional software features. Apple's response includes multiple troubleshooting steps ranging from network resets to complete device restoration, demonstrating the complex dependency chains that AI features introduce to mobile products. The timing of this issue, occurring just as iPhone 17 models reach consumers, underscores the critical importance of stress-testing AI feature delivery systems before major product launches.
